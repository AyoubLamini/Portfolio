### Priority Issues

- **[P1] Color System & Contrast:**
  - *Why it matters:* Multiple neon accent colors create high visual fatigue and read as unprofessional. Furthermore, the body/muted text contrast ratio of `#6b7280` on `#050508` is only 2.8:1, failing the WCAG AA minimum contrast of 4.5:1, making the site unreadable for visually impaired users.
  - *Fix:* Refactor styles to follow the restrained zinc/slate and Calm Indigo palette defined in `DESIGN.md`.
  - *Suggested command:* `/impeccable colorize`

- **[P1] Cyberpunk Visual Noise & AI tells:**
  - *Why it matters:* Glows, scanlines, custom cursors, and unneeded particles distract recruiters and create a cheap, template-like impression.
  - *Fix:* Strip all glows (`glow-text`), glitches (`glitch-text`), and particles. Remove the custom cursor completely.
  - *Suggested command:* `/impeccable quieter`

- **[P1] Performance & Layout Thrash:**
  - *Why it matters:* Animating layout properties like `width` and `height` forces layout calculations and introduces rendering jank. Custom cursor scripts add rendering lag.
  - *Fix:* Remove the custom cursor script and replace height/width transitions in `WebProjectsCarousel.tsx` with transforms or grid-template-rows transitions.
  - *Suggested command:* `/impeccable optimize`

- **[P2] Font Scaffolding:**
  - *Why it matters:* Space Grotesk is on the reflex-reject list and is a common AI default.
  - *Fix:* Change the typography system to utilize `Geist Sans` for both body and display headers, with negative letter-spacing for headlines.
  - *Suggested command:* `/impeccable typeset`

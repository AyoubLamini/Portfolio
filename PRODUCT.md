# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Technical recruiters, hiring managers, engineering leads, and potential clients. They scan portfolios quickly, seeking clear evidence of engineering quality, frontend architecture competence, product thinking, and meticulous attention to detail.

## Product Purpose
Introduce Ayoub Lamini as a Software Engineer & Web Developer. Showcase a solid systems programming background (C/C++) combined with modern full-stack web expertise (Next.js, TypeScript). Provide clear access to project details, source code, resume, and contact methods. Success is defined by visitors immediately recognizing a professional, competent engineer who builds polished, highly performant products.

## Brand Personality
Precise, intentional, and restrained. The aesthetic mimics a premium SaaS product or a high-end software studio—calm, typographic, and clean, rather than flashy, futuristic, or gamified.

## Anti-references
- AI-generated portfolio templates (generic card grids, floating blobs, overused blue-to-purple gradients, excessive glassmorphism).
- Overly flashy developer sites (cyberpunk/hacker themes, heavy neon colors, glowing text, scanlines, glitch effects).
- Decorative, non-functional animations that distract from content, slow down load times, or compromise readability.
- Dribbble-style designs that prioritize visual spectacle over usability, accessibility, and clean alignment.

## Design Principles
- **Engineering is the Aesthetic:** Every component, layout grid, and interactive state is designed to showcase engineering craftsmanship, clean layout systems, and performance. No decorative fluff.
- **Quiet Precision:** Rely on excellent typography, strict spacing rules, and a highly focused color palette. Visual interest comes from impeccable alignment and premium detailing, not novelty.
- **Usability-First Motion:** Animations are subtle, smooth, and functional. They guide the user's attention to state changes or interactive elements, never delaying task completion or causing distraction.
- **Earned Trust:** Design components with the quality of top-tier SaaS applications (e.g., Stripe, Linear, Vercel). The UI should disappear, allowing Ayoub's projects and skills to speak for themselves.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- strict WCAG AA/AAA compliance for color contrast (ink text on background).
- Comprehensive keyboard navigation and visible focus indicators.
- Respect for user system preferences (e.g. `prefers-reduced-motion` to instantly bypass animations).
- Semantic HTML and accessibility landmarks throughout.
